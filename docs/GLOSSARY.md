## Disambiguation

| Confused pair | Correct reading |
|---------------|-----------------|
| **CCM** vs **CA** | CCM = TB/HIV Country Coordinating Mechanism. CA = Consiliul Audiovizualului (media regulator). |
| **CNPDC** vs **CNPD** | Prefer **CNPDC**; CNPD appears as a short/variant rendering. |
| **ISS** vs **CNAPSS** | ISS hosts/publishes; CNAPSS is the accreditation council for social-service providers. |
| **ASP** vs **RSON** | ASP is the agency; RSON is the non-commercial org register extract. |
